## Inside the Instantly Pricing Wall: What $47 Actually Buys

<AnswerCapsule>
Instantly splits email sending, lead data credits, and inbox placement tests into three separate subscriptions. The $47 Growth tier limits your account to 1,000 fixed uploaded contacts across all campaigns and drops full Unibox CRM access after a 14-day trial, pushing active teams into the $107 Hypergrowth tier.
</AnswerCapsule>

Most comparisons list Instantly's $47 entry price next to competitors and stop there. That skips the rules written inside [Instantly's Help Center](https://help.instantly.ai/en/articles/10273259-instantly-plans-overview).

**Wall 1: The fixed 1,000-contact lock-in.** Growth ($47 monthly, or $37.60 billed annually) allows unlimited connected inboxes and warmup, alongside a 5,000-email monthly send ceiling. Crucially, the 1,000 uploaded contacts rule is a fixed cap across all campaigns rather than a monthly allowance. Once 1,000 leads sit inside your workspace, you cannot upload lead #1,001 unless you delete earlier prospect records or upgrade to Hypergrowth ($107 monthly, or $85.58 annually).

**Wall 2: The split-meter stack.** Sending emails, pulling contact records, and monitoring folder placement each carry separate monthly price tags on [Instantly's pricing page](https://instantly.ai/pricing):

| Subscription Layer | Entry Tier | Mid Tier | Scale / Upper Tier | Key Limit to Watch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Outbound (Sending)** | Growth: $47/mo ($37.60 annual) | Hypergrowth: $107/mo ($85.58 annual) | Light Speed: $358/mo ($286.30 annual) | Growth caps at 1,000 contacts and 5,000 sends |
| **Instantly Credits (Data & AI)** | Nano: $9/mo (150 credits); Growth: $47/mo (1,500 credits) | Supersonic: $97/mo (5,000 credits) | Hyper Credits: From $197/mo (10k credits) up to $1,700/mo | Credits-only users without Outbound can upload just 250 leads |
| **Bundles (10% Off Combined)** | Starter: $94/mo ($85 annual) | Scale: $194/mo ($175 annual) | Agency: $555/mo ($500 annual) | Scale Bundle combines 100k sends with 5,000 data credits |
| **Inbox Placement Add-On** | Growth Placement: $47/mo | Hypergrowth Placement: $97/mo | Included in neither Starter nor Scale Bundles | Automated spam placement tests cost extra |

Add infrastructure purchases if you source mailboxes through Instantly rather than setting up domains yourself. Domains run $15 a year. Instantly Airmail accounts run $4 monthly per inbox, Done-For-You Google seats run $5 monthly, and pre-warmed Google seats run $10 monthly. A team needing 25,000 contact slots alongside full Unibox CRM replies, 5,000 monthly data credits, and automated placement testing hits $291 to $301 monthly before buying a single mailbox.

**Wall 3: The Unibox CRM cutoff.** Instantly gives new workspaces a 14-day CRM trial. When that window closes on the $47 Growth tier, full CRM-integrated Unibox management locks behind Hypergrowth ($107 monthly). Anyone who evaluates instantly pricing alternatives hits this barrier as soon as replies start arriving across five or ten domains.

When should you stay on Instantly? Stay if you rely on its 450M+ B2B lead database and AI Web Researcher inside one login, or if you need Light Speed ($358 monthly) for its built-in SISR private IP sharding network at 500,000 sends per month.

## How to Migrate Off Instantly Without Losing Sender Reputation

<AnswerCapsule>
Migrating off Instantly preserves inbox placement if you reconnect your exact existing Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 addresses over OAuth, export an active suppression list first, and maintain a five-day warmup overlap at flat daily volume before ramping sends.
</AnswerCapsule>

Your domain reputation lives with mailbox providers and DNS records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), not inside Instantly's software. Follow four steps to switch without triggering [Google's Email Sender Guidelines](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126) spam safeguards (keeping user-reported spam under 0.1% and safely away from the 0.3% cutoff documented in [Google's Sender FAQ](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14229414)):

1. **Export contacts and build a suppression list.** Download all unsubscribed, bounced, replied, and active-sequence contacts from Instantly before canceling. Upload that global do-not-contact file into your new platform before importing fresh leads so you never double-email prospects mid-thread.
2. **Re-authenticate the identical sender addresses.** Connect your existing secondary domains and mailboxes to your new sequencer over OAuth or SMTP/IMAP using the exact same From addresses and display names. Wait to cancel Instantly until accounts are connected and active campaigns are paused cleanly.
3. **Update custom tracking domain CNAMEs.** Point each custom tracking subdomain (for example `track.yourdomain.com`) to your new platform's CNAME target and verify SSL issuance before resuming links or open tracking.
4. **Hold daily volume steady for five days.** Turn off Instantly warmup at the exact moment you enable your new tool's warmup network. Keep campaign daily caps unchanged for five days while watching bounce plus spam rates in Google Postmaster Tools, then step volume upward by 10% to 20% weekly once clean metrics hold. (InvokeIQ Growth and Scale plans include Done-For-You migration assistance to handle exports and inbox onboarding.)
