Why do 90% of short-form videos fail to get traction? Because amateur editors simply add auto-generated captions and call it a day. 

At Explode Labs, we treat motion graphics as an engineering discipline. Every frame, sound wave, and pixel transition is calibrated around human visual psychology:

#### 1. The 2-Second Hook Pattern Interrupt
The human brain scrolls past social media posts in 400 milliseconds. To hold attention, we deploy immediate visual friction: subtle camera snap-zooms, floating animated iconography, sound-designed whooshes, and high-contrast headline motion that forces the viewer's thumb to stop.

#### 2. Custom Kinetic Typography (Not Lazy Auto-Captions)
Auto-generated yellow subtitles are everywhere and viewers have developed subconscious blindness to them. We design custom kinetic typography tailored to your brand:
*   Dynamic word-by-word highlighting synced to speech cadence.
*   Contextual animated icons that replace spoken nouns (e.g., an animated credit card icon when you say "revenue").
*   Custom bounding boxes and subtle drop shadows that guarantee 100% legibility over any background footage.

#### 3. Cinema-Grade Dual-Layer Sound Design
Over 70% of high-retention video performance comes down to audio. We build a custom soundscape for every single clip:
*   **Foley Layer:** Page flips, keyboard clicks, paper tearing, and subtle mechanical clicks synced to on-screen motion.
*   **Spatial Accents:** Low-frequency sub-bass drops on punchlines, cinematic risers before key transitions, and whooshes that guide the viewer's eye across the frame.
*   **Audio Mastering:** Loudness normalization (mastered to -14 LUFS for Reels/TikTok/YouTube) with vocal isolation and background noise cleanup.

#### 4. 2D/3D Graphic Overlays & Screen-Space Tracking
When you mention a concept, software feature, or metric, we don't just put text on screen—we track 3D charts, glowing arrows, animated interface mockups, and illustrative overlays directly onto your footage using Adobe After Effects and Cinema 4D.

#### 5. DaVinci Resolve ACES Color Grading
Raw footage shot on iPhones or mirrorless cameras often looks flat, muddy, or inconsistent under artificial lighting. We process every clip through professional DaVinci Resolve color pipelines, balancing skin tones, lifting contrast, and applying custom film emulation LUTs to ensure you look like an authority in your space.
