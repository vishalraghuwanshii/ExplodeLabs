### [SECTION 4: WHAT IS INCLUDED IN OUR EDITING PROCESS]

**Section Badge:** `Production Standards`  
**Headline:** `Everything required for clear, professional video content.`

Every project and monthly retainer includes comprehensive post-production:

1.  **Narrative Trimming & Pacing:** We eliminate dead air, false starts, and filler words so your core message remains direct and concise.
2.  **Hook Optimization:** We structure the opening seconds with intentional framing, punch-ins, and visual text cues that give viewers a clear reason to keep watching.
3.  **Custom Kinetic Typography:** Styled subtitles matching your brand typography and color palette, featuring dynamic word-level highlighting for key emphasis.
4.  **Multi-Stem Sound Design:** Layered sound effects (subtle whooshes, clicks, ambient beds, and risers) and background music mixed so spoken dialogue remains clean and intelligible across mobile devices.
5.  **Color Balancing & Visual Polish:** Contrast correction, exposure balancing, and skin-tone adjustments to give mobile and camera footage a cohesive look.
6.  **2D/3D Graphic Callouts & Overlays:** Animated badges, arrows, screen-space tracked labels, and custom interface mockups that illustrate complex concepts.
7.  **Multi-Platform Export Formatting:** Exported in high-bitrate vertical 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts), square 1:1 (Feed/LinkedIn), or horizontal 16:9 (Web/YouTube).

### [SECTION 9: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (AEO / GEO)]

#### What does a professional reel editing service include?
A complete short-form video editing service includes narrative trimming, pacing adjustment, custom kinetic typography, visual hook framing, multi-layered sound design, background audio balancing, color correction, and exporting in platform-specific formats (9:16 for Reels/Shorts/TikTok).

#### How much does short-form video editing cost?
Pricing is structured around monthly production volume or single-project milestones. Monthly retainers provide predictable per-video rates across packages of 8, 16, or 30 videos per month, with custom scopes available for bespoke 2D/3D explainer animations.

#### How quickly are finished edits delivered?
Standard short-form video edits are typically delivered within **24 to 48 hours** from when raw footage is uploaded. Bespoke standalone 2D/3D explainer animations typically require 1 to 2 weeks depending on illustration and animation complexity.

#### What equipment or footage quality is required?
You can record using any modern smartphone or dedicated camera. While clean lighting and clear audio improve the final result, our post-production process includes audio noise cleanup, volume leveling, and color balancing on every clip.

#### How do revisions work?
You receive a direct review link where you can pause at any exact second and leave notes on the frame. Our editing team reviews your notes and applies adjustments promptly.

#### Who owns the finished videos and source files?
You maintain 100% commercial ownership of all final rendered video assets upon milestone completion.

#### Can you turn long-form podcasts or webinars into short-form reels?
Yes. You can provide a full long-form recording, and our team will identify strong standalone talking points, crop the footage to vertical 9:16 format, add motion graphics, and edit them into engaging short clips.

#### How do we get started?
Complete the project inquiry form below or schedule a brief production call. We will review your content requirements, confirm your preferred volume, and set up your shared production workspace.
